Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Know what to expect before connecting a wallet.

Read the full guide ↗
Do I need a wallet to explore?

No. You can browse public launches, token profiles, fee policies, and recorded activity first. Connect a wallet when you need its balances or a signing action.

Which network does the app use?

funded.vip runs on Solana. The network the app is using is shown in the label at the top of every page.

Are rewards paid after every trade?

A trade can trigger processing of ready work. Payment still depends on the program’s eligibility, cutoff, verified funding, minimums, and any required approvals. Ranked bonuses keep their windows and cannot be awarded before rankings close.

Which token does the protocol buy and burn?

The protocol buyback flow purchases and burns $FUNDED, rather than every launched token. An allocated amount is not proof of a completed buyback; use the confirmed purchase and burn receipts.

Where can I see a token’s fee split?

Open its token page to inspect the saved policy and available fee records. The capital-flow page explains the current policy. Existing launches and recorded settlements retain their saved rates.

What am I approving with my wallet?

Review the action, network, token, destination, costs, and amount in the app and your wallet. Transaction fees and other displayed costs can apply. The platform does not ask for your seed phrase or private key.
