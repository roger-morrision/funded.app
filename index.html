    Frequently asked

    Before you launch

    Know what you can earn, which asset you receive, and whether you need to claim it.

    SOLShares of collected creator fees
    Coin tokensA separate reserve of the launched coin
    Explore rewards →
    Which rewards are SOL, and which are coin tokens?

    Collected creator fees are paid in SOL to the destinations selected at launch: the creator wallet, eligible coin holders, and a verified X account. Qualified referrers can also earn SOL. A community airdrop uses tokens of the launched coin reserved at launch for eligible $FUNDED holders. Always check the asset shown on a reward before claiming.

    How are collected creator fees allocated?

    For each finalized collection, 80% follows the creator's launch-time split between their wallet, coin holders, and an eligible X account. The other 20% funds operations (10%), referrals (3%), community (2%), and $FUNDED buyback and burn (5%). This is the split for new launches. Existing coins keep their recorded split. See the fee split.

    How do coin holders receive SOL?

    Hold a coin whose launch policy gives a share of creator fees to holders. After fees are collected and a complete eligible-wallet snapshot is finalized, SOL is sent automatically to eligible wallets; there is no holder claim button. Amounts below the payout minimum roll into a later cycle. Check holder rewards and payments.

    How do creators, X accounts, and referrers receive SOL?
    • Creator: the selected wallet receives its share automatically after verified fee collection and settlement.
    • X account: sign in with the named X account, choose a ready reward, verify the receiving wallet, and complete the claim.
    • Referrer: connect the earning wallet, sign the claim request, then execute each available payout.
    How do I receive launched coin tokens?

    A launch reserves at least 3% of its coin supply for the community airdrop. Eligible $FUNDED holders are determined by that launch's migration snapshot. Once the token vault, snapshot, and claim proof are verified, connect the eligible wallet and claim during the displayed window in Airdrops. Holding $FUNDED after the snapshot alone does not create an allocation.

    Why can an allocation appear before I receive anything?

    An allocation describes where a reward should go; it is not a payment. Fee collection, eligibility, funding, and any required claim must finish first. A reward is marked paid only after a finalized transaction and recipient receipt are verified. Check payment history for completed payouts.

    Are launches and market figures verified?

    Launches appear after a Solana mint is confirmed. Market figures show available indexed observations and may be partial or unavailable.

    Does funded.vip control my wallet?

    No. Your wallet approves signing. Review the transaction and fee route before you approve it. Never share your seed phrase or private key.

    What changes when a coin migrates?

    Trading moves from the Pump curve to a pool. Migration also sets the snapshot used to determine eligibility for that coin's community token airdrop. Check Airdrops for the verified snapshot and claim status.

    Where can I inspect the fee and burn policy?

    Open the fee split for the published route and the $FUNDED page for buyback and burn receipts.

    Something looks wrong. Where can I ask?

    Open Help in the lower corner for guidance, then compare the mint, wallet, and transaction signature with their linked Solana records.

    Referral guide

    Know how rewards work

    Published policy
    When do rewards become payable?

    Only after qualified creator-fee revenue is collected by the verified router, finalized on Solana, and indexed. Creating an account or recruiting alone does not create a reward.

    What happens when a level is missing?

    The missing level remains in the community growth reserve. It is not redistributed to another inviter.

    Are rewards guaranteed?

    No. The displayed percentages are policy rates, not income promises. Actual rewards depend on qualified creator activity and collected revenue.

    What if a collection is not final?

    No referral reward is created from an unverified or failed collection. A claim becomes available only after the indexed collection is finalized.
