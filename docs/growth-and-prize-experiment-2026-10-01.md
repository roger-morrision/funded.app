# Growth and retention experiment — 2026-10-01

## Product loop

1. A creator opens Create and enters a name, ticker, and image before choosing optional setup depth.
2. The creator reviews the exact network, cost, recipient, supply allocation, and fee route before signing.
3. A confirmed coin has a canonical token page the creator can share manually. Visitors can inspect its source facts and save it without buying.
4. A returning visitor sees watched coins, opted-in creator updates, and receipt-backed reward events. A payout is shown as paid only after finalized proof.

The existing app has watchlists, creator follows, reward proof views, share cards, and local in-app alert polling. This change makes the launch-first fields and existing community alert navigation easier to reach. The current checkout's coin-share composer offers native sharing when available and a link-copy fallback. It does not add background delivery, Mainnet payments, or a prize system.

## Four-week cohort test

Recruit a small opt-in group of creators with established communities. Record the release build, entry channel, and network for each test. Establish a baseline before claiming lift. Do not count test wallets, synthetic fixtures, or paid incentive traffic as organic retention.

| Measure | Definition | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft-to-review completion | Unique consenting participants who reach a valid launch review / participants who start a draft | Find launch friction; inspect failures by step and device. |
| D7 retained activation | Activated participants who return on day 7 and view a followed coin, creator update, or verified reward / activated participants eligible for day-7 observation | Primary retention measure; cohort by acquisition source. |
| Creator follow-through | Pilot creators who publish a useful second update within 14 days / creators who published a first update | Test whether creator pages support an ongoing community. |
| Share-to-retained visitor | Retained activated visitors arriving from a creator's canonical token link / unique visitors from those links | Test whether sharing brings useful repeat use. |
| Reward trust | Participants who can correctly distinguish published, allocated, and paid in a short usability task | Guard against misleading reward claims. |

The existing roadmap proposes initial targets of 25% D7 retention and 30% creator follow-through. These are hypotheses, not observed results. Use consented, purpose-limited measurement and report the sample size, exclusions, and uncertainty. The current device-local diagnostics cannot measure unique users or retention across devices.

### Pilot operation and interpretation

The Portfolio page now offers a separate opt-in pilot record. It stores a random local participant ID, a selected entry source, event names, and UTC timestamps on that device only. It does not upload wallet addresses, social handles, post text, or browsing URLs. Disabling the pilot deletes the record; the participant must explicitly export JSON for a researcher to use it. Test and synthetic sources are excluded from the cohort summary. To summarize consented exports, run `node scripts/summarize-pilot-metrics.mjs <export1.json> <export2.json> ...`.

For this pilot, activation is the first completed launch review, saved coin, or followed creator. A day-7 return is a view of a watched coin, followed creator, or verified reward on the seventh UTC calendar day after activation. The denominator includes only devices with the full day-7 observation window. Creator follow-through is a second successfully published update within 14 days of the first; the denominator includes only records observed for the full 14 days. Draft completion requires a draft start and a later valid review. These records are local device observations, **not unique people or cross-device retention**. Recruit and consent real pilot participants separately; no pilot participants or measured lift are claimed here.

### Friction fixes in this checkout

The sidebar presents four primary destinations, with secondary pages under More. A visitor can browse verified launches and draft a coin name, ticker, and image before wallet connection. Optional story fields, launch tiers, and custom fee splits sit behind disclosures. The launch cost text distinguishes zero platform fee from network fees, developer buy, and burn costs. Reward evidence still distinguishes published, collected, allocated, available, and paid. Trade status links the transaction signature, waits for finalization before calling a trade complete, and warns against retrying an uncertain submission. The Explore payer label no longer implies endorsement. Chat explains that wallet control does not verify project affiliation; reporting and posting limits remain in place. Watched reward alerts now cover only finalized holder payments backed by a verified fee source and verified buyback burns, grouped at most once per coin and event kind per day. The raw-volume trader leaderboard tab is removed from the main ranking view. Home now shows both jackpot amounts from the same public Devnet status response as Rewards; unavailable funding is never displayed as a prize. The Rewards overview places a compact two-amount snapshot near the top, with a link to the detailed cards and their expandable rules, funding, and draw-proof view. Inactive countdowns are labeled as UTC accounting windows with no scheduled draw. New finalized winner receipts can trigger an opt-in in-app notice while the page is open, even after a round closes.

The optional device-local pilot now records jackpot Home visibility, opening Rewards from the Home card, Rewards visibility, and rules views only after consent. Synthetic test sources are excluded from the cohort summary. These event counts describe interaction with a preview; they are not evidence of acquisition lift, unique people, or jackpot participation. A pilot should compare useful day-7 returns and creator follow-through as well as these discovery counts, and review spam or wash-trading signals before any growth claim.

## Prize decision

No purchase-, trade-, holding-, launch-, or fee-weighted chance prize is enabled. The Devnet community reserve is an allocated and funded vault, not an approved prize budget or payable community program. The inactive 24-hour jackpot prototype is now hidden by default behind `VITE_JACKPOT_ENABLED=false`; its API route returns 404 while disabled. Enabling the flag only exposes the prototype: its funded prize is zero, entries are unavailable, and no payout is sent. Home instead features recorded coin-holder SOL cycles and per-launch $FUNDED-holder token allocations, with countdowns only when a verified schedule or active claim expiry exists.

The requested creator design uses 1% of **funded.vip's 20% share** of collected Pump creator fees (equivalent to 0.2% of gross fees), with one ticket per unique, finalized launch in the UTC day. The trader design uses 5% of **funded.vip's collected app trading fees**, with one ticket per unique, finalized trade that paid the app fee. The model uses integer lamports and requires finalized source receipts. A future draw must use publicly verifiable entropy independent of the operator and publish its eligible entry set. A winner receipt is marked paid only after a matching finalized SOL transfer with verified balance delta.

The existing creator settlement fully allocates its funded.vip share. No jackpot allocation was taken from operations, referrals, community, or buyback, and no historical policy snapshot was changed. The current app-fee wallet has no complete, wallet-attributed finalized fee ledger. Therefore neither pool can truthfully report fee funding or eligible entries. The revised Devnet payout-leg test used 0.004 SOL from the rotated QA referrer, confirmed a refund canary, funded separate temporary creator and trader vaults including transaction fees, and finalized 0.001 SOL to the QA creator and 0.002 SOL to the QA claimant. Finalized payout signatures are `25K3Q9rEu3weHE5RPJv5FYxQDoi1RCp3VbFx3hKtE6cZDPm5cN4HzKPf3jCLVrxQJ5oFSBiQ2Vny7K7yvuXkz2To` and `2SV5uNFTv4q5zxSMzfUXHW1TmKyWPSVY2Ls4CGBTpWjt3qtZeeX47TNmMf53niS4z561d3juEXx2e1EJqnEBkpp7`; both temporary vaults and the temporary source closed to zero after the refund. This proves the isolated funding and payout legs, with predetermined QA recipients. It does not prove app-fee funding, entry indexing, a real winner draw, or a scheduled jackpot. An earlier manual attempt used ephemeral keys without a refund path and left residual Devnet SOL inaccessible after exit; the revised script now requires an external refund address before creating keys.

On the same Devnet QA wallets, a finalized `$FUNDED` transfer (`2W7s2SRTMxG55wG2j1oXjtNrUEdaBu3VVJsB965LvuGx3S5XQwFhVzYsxyQR28zhm5EFZFtc6M1VcKCMqAmD3Ten`) moved 992.001 tokens to the creator and 101 tokens to the claimant, leaving them at 1,000 and 1,100 tokens at verification time. The source and recipient base-unit deltas matched the transfer and mint supply was unchanged. A separate live launch and buy/sell journey reached creator-fee collection, where the local server stopped it because its fee-router ProgramData hash allowlist did not match the current Devnet program. The local allowlist still needs independent approval before that flow is retried. The creator Boost launch also remains short of its 25,000 `$FUNDED` requirement. These are remaining funding and readiness blockers, not completed jackpot flows.

Before a funded Devnet jackpot round, add a versioned prospective creator split, dedicated jackpot vault, a persistent finalized fee and entry index, a replay-safe scheduler, and independently verifiable draw proof. The current code can verify individual finalized launch and trade-entry transactions plus an exact trader-fee funding transfer into a specified vault, but it does not create a vault, persist or schedule entries, or prove a creator jackpot allocation. Mainnet requires further custody and program review. Worldwide eligibility with paid activity increasing odds requires jurisdiction-specific legal review; a worldwide live switch is not present. The [FTC says purchase-required sweepstakes are illegal in the US](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business), and the [UK Gambling Commission warns that draws without a compliant free route may be illegal lotteries](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-and-players/guide/page/free-draws-and-prize-competitions).

If the core loop improves retention, a separate fixed-budget free-entry draw can be considered only after jurisdiction-specific legal review and published rules covering eligibility, geographic and age limits, free entry parity, winner selection, odds, prize custody, payout, taxes, abuse handling, and shutdown. Paid activity must not increase entry or odds. An independent audit of selection and verified funding/payment receipts would precede any user-facing promise. Keep the experiment off until those gates pass.

The browser fixture checked Home and Rewards amounts, inactive mobile copy, rules, the countdown, receipt replay, opt-in notices, and reduced motion. It did not prove a fee-funded round or automatic payout.
- The broad workspace UI suite passed all 229 checks before exposing the community controls. Later runs had one timeout each in different data-dependent home or burn views; the focused changed-control check passed. The cause of those timeouts remains unverified, so a stable broad-suite run is still needed before release.
