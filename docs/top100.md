# Top 100

Open **Top 100** in the main navigation, or visit `https://funded.vip/#top100`.

Existing `#top300` bookmarks open Top 100.

The index ranks up to 100 tokens in funded.vip's verified directory, including supported paid external listings. It is not a list of all Solana tokens.

## Rankings

| View | Order | Eligible tokens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Featured · paid priority (default) | Active boost → paid listing → Premier → Pro → 24h MC growth → 24h volume → 24h holder growth → 24h calls/chat → social channels → 24h trades → mint | All verified directory tokens; unavailable values last at each priority step |
| 24h MC growth | Net USD market-cap increase descending | Reliable opening and current valuations available |
| Boosted · sponsored | Active paid multiplier descending, then market cap | At least one active verified boost |
| 24h volume | Observed USD volume descending, then market cap | Recent activity snapshot and SOL/USD quote available |
| 24h trades | Observed trade count descending, then market cap | Recent activity snapshot available |

Featured uses the verified launch promotion receipt or a verified paid-listing receipt, not an unverified tier label. Active verified boosts take precedence, ordered by their current summed multiplier. Every paid-priority or boost-priority row is labeled. Paid listings come before Premier, then Pro. Each later criterion breaks ties only after all earlier criteria tie; the numeric market views remain independent of these payments. This improves visibility without guaranteeing a place in the first 100 or investment performance. Featured priority is separate from the 48-hour Home placement window.

Equal values use the mint address as a stable tie-breaker. Tokens are deduplicated by mint. The first 100 are selected before filters; filtering preserves their original ranks. Results paginate in groups of 25. Search accepts a name, symbol or mint; filters cover stage, launch tier, paid listings, market cap and known launch age.

Boost purchases use the existing checkout, package prices, finalized payment verification, expiry and payment-recovery flow. Active package multipliers add together. A combined multiplier of 500 or more highlights the token name in gold. Boosts affect Featured and Boosted; they do not affect 24h MC growth, Volume or Trades rankings. Each package runs for its purchased 12 or 24 hours from confirmation. Buying again adds multipliers while packs overlap and can extend the last expiry; it does not extend an older package or keep the summed multiplier active until the last package ends. The table shows time to the next multiplier change and final expiry. A local timer recalculates ranks at the next expiry, without waiting for the next server refresh. Expired boosts disappear from Boosted and lose their Featured priority. Launch promotion tiers and the separate 48-hour Home Featured placement remain distinct from active boosts.

## Rolling 24-hour metrics

- Market-cap growth is current USD valuation minus the opening USD valuation, not the current cap or percentage price change. Each valuation uses the token supply and SOL/USD quote observed at that time.
- Holder growth is the change in distinct funded wallet owners from complete token-account lists. Program-derived owners/vaults are excluded consistently. Sampled lower bounds cannot establish net growth.
- Volume and trade counts include observed trades in the rolling last 24 hours. Calls/chat count visible retained posts from that window, with each call counted once. Hidden/deleted posts are excluded. The chat store retains at most 100 posts per token, so this is a retained count, not a guarantee of complete discussion history.
- Social count is the number of distinct linked X, Telegram and Discord platforms (0–3), not followers or proof of account ownership. It is not a 24-hour activity metric.

Negative growth stays negative. Known losses still precede missing data at the same priority step. Missing opening values are never zero or since-launch growth.

Server market scans persist verified USD valuations; complete token-account reads persist holder counts. Observations are collected through existing bounded read flows, not from browser-submitted values. Five-minute samples retain 26 hours plus the latest reading, scoped by network and mint. An opening sample must be at or before the rolling cutoff and no more than ten minutes older; the current reading must be less than five minutes old. Quiet tokens, new deployments, failed reads or incomplete account scans may lack sufficient history and display **—**. This sampling is approximate to the stated opening tolerance, not exact block-at-cutoff accounting. At least 24 hours of appropriately timed observations are needed before new history can provide growth.

`GET /api/top100/metrics` returns network-scoped growth and retained community counts, cached for 15 seconds. It exposes no message text or wallet-owner lists. The table shows current MC separately for context and filtering; it is not used as Featured's MC-growth criterion.

## Data and availability

Directory pages contain 50 records each. Top 100 reads beyond Explore's ordinary first 40 records, reuses feed account snapshots and cached market activity, and batches missing RPC account reads in groups of at most 100. Paging stops at the directory end or the protective 10,000-record ceiling. Incomplete coverage is disclosed; it must not be described as the entire Solana market. Concurrent refreshes reuse the existing market loader. Activity scans retain the existing bounded scan budget. Automatic refresh runs only while this route is active and visible, every 60 seconds; manual Refresh retries sooner.

Market caps use confirmed curve or migrated pool data and the current SOL/USD quote. A completed curve is not a substitute for a migrated pool valuation. Missing values appear as **—**, not zero. Activity snapshots older than five minutes are withheld. Partial observed volume/trade counts are prefixed with **≥**. An external token's paid-listing date is not presented as its launch age. Unpriced directory tokens are counted but cannot receive a current valuation; tokens without a reliable growth baseline cannot receive a 24h MC growth rank. Source failures retain last loaded data with a retry warning; unavailable boosts are not presented as verified zero boosts.

The table has semantic column headers, horizontal scrolling on small screens, full symbols, uncropped logos, 44-pixel controls and keyboard focus states. No wallet connection is required to browse rankings.
