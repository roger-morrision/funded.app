# Daily bonus and protocol fee policy

## Recipient delivery and timing

Daily bonuses are **automatic SOL transfers**: winners do not claim or sign a recipient transaction. The worker checks every five minutes for completed UTC days and pending payouts. A day closes at **00:00 UTC** (07:00 Bangkok); the countdown starts verification, not an immediate payment guarantee. Exact source funding is still required after creator settlement/funding approval. Incomplete history, missing funding, reconciliation or an unavailable worker can delay payment.

Ordinary referral-network transfers are separate: they require creator approval but no recipient claim. X SOL and community token airdrops require recipient claim actions.

## Dashboard and payment history

### Five-minute Devnet QA

`FUNDED_DEVNET_QA_BONUS_PERIOD_SECONDS=300` requires Devnet, `FUNDED_DEVNET_QA_MINIMUMS_ENABLED=true`, and one to eight disposable mints in `FUNDED_DEVNET_QA_BONUS_MINTS`. No other shortened period is accepted. Unset the period or set it to `86400` to restore daily windows. Mainnet rejects the QA configuration.

QA windows align to UTC five-minute boundaries and use distinct keys such as `qa5m:2026-10-18T10:15:00Z@<cohort-hash>`. Only selected test mints contribute rankings and sources. The cohort hash binds the selected mint list, so changing test coins cannot reuse an immutable window from another cohort. The worker checks every 30 seconds; finalized indexing, settlement, exact source funding and program checks still apply. Home, Capital flow and history expose the QA schedule. A completed window is not a payment guarantee.

Existing daily snapshots and payment manifests are preserved. A collection assigned to a payout cannot fund a second window or the later daily snapshot. Consumed sources are explicitly excluded from later funding snapshots while their legitimate ranking evidence can remain. Missing ranks retain funds; tests do not redistribute them to fabricate full payment coverage.

Home cards animate actual category allocations from verified indexed settlements; they do not simulate continuously earned income. `GET /api/daily-bonus/dashboard` supplies server time, cutoff, current pool, paginated history and all-time top-ten recipients across **all recorded snapshots**, rather than only the latest 30 snapshots in `GET /api/daily-bonus`.

A combined payment can cover multiple categories. Its verified net receipt is attributed proportionally with exact lamport rounding and counted once. Gross allocations and received totals are not added together. Repeated aliases are deduplicated; missing net evidence, conflicting proofs and failed snapshot integrity are reported as unavailable and excluded from rankings. Rewards History and Analytics separately verify finalized instructions, accounts and recipient balance deltas. An empty history does not mean an allocation was paid.

## Allocation, eligibility and execution

The current configuration allocates gross collected creator fees as 80% creator-directed, 8% Operations & marketing, 3% referral network, 4% Daily bonus, and 5% $FUNDED buyback. In `config/protocol-fee-split.js`, the values are percentages of the protocol's 20% share: 40 / 15 / 20 / 25. Existing mint policies and their historical obligations retain their recorded rates. A saved admin policy takes precedence over configuration; publishing the 40/20 operations/community values through the authenticated admin editor is necessary if an earlier override exists.

`config/daily-bonus.js` defines a completed UTC calendar day, 00:00–00:00. The base community allocation for launches that recorded daily bonus policy version 1 is split into three equal budgets. The top three creators rank by finalized settled creator fees, direct referrers by those referred creators' settled fees, and traders by finalized Pump/PumpSwap buy and sell SOL volume. Each category pays ranks 1/2/3 at 50/30/20. Ties use ascending wallet address. One wallet may qualify in several categories; each source mint combines its awards into one Merkle leaf. Integer rounding is deterministic. Empty ranks retain their portion in the source reward vault. Unmatched ordinary referral levels and old community allocations are not daily bonus funding sources.

New snapshots use **settlement-time-v1**: creator and direct-referrer scores count only fees whose matching settlement finalized inside the window, including fees collected earlier. A settlement at the start is included; one at the end belongs to the next window. Trades retain their own finalized execution time. Unsettled collections are listed as not eligible and do not hold unrelated settled sources. An unknown finalized settlement still waits for its exact collection proof and attribution; it cannot silently disappear from rankings. Historical snapshots without this version retain collection-time eligibility and their original hashes.

The snapshot reader independently verifies Devnet genesis, finalized cutoff time, collections, settlements, frozen direct-referral identities, and successful program-emitted trade events. It paginates curve, pool and policy settlement history to the window boundary. Missing transactions, truncated logs, unresolved times, or unfinished pagination delay the snapshot. Rankings describe verified trading volume; they do not establish that unrelated wallets have independent owners or that trades are free of coordinated activity.

Late evidence pauses further distribution until an administrator reviews a hash-bound reconciliation. A completely unused snapshot may be replaced with an archived audit revision. Prepared or paid windows permit only additive evidence: original ranks, scores, source manifests, retained balances and receipts are preserved, and new unassigned funding pays those frozen winners. Review re-reads finalized payment accounts and their exact cycle roots, recipients and amounts. Modified or removed original evidence fails closed. The review and commit hashes must agree; a reason and authenticated operator are recorded. Repeating execution recovers the same source cycles and never assigns a source twice.

Each community source must first be funded by the existing creator-signed V3 community funding flow. The executor verifies the exact finalized withdrawal, category, recipient vault, amount and mint policy before creating a reserved reward cycle. It uses the existing V3 proof-gated SOL reward instructions, one deterministic cycle per source collection and UTC day. Replays recover the same cycle and payment accounts. Failed or unfunded sources retain their allocation; they cannot borrow holder/X funding or another day's source. A payer covers transaction and account costs separately.

`GET /api/daily-bonus` exposes rankings, immutable snapshot hashes, source funding state and finalized payment evidence. Authenticated keeper requests can POST a `day` to `/api/daily-bonus/snapshot` and `/api/daily-bonus/execute`. `FUNDED_DAILY_BONUS_ENABLED=true` enables the five-minute maintenance check for completed daily snapshots; `FUNDED_DAILY_BONUS_PAYOUT_ENABLED=true` separately permits signed payouts after verified community funding. Both flags default off, and execution is restricted to Devnet and the reviewed V3 bytecode. The worker waits for a launch that recorded the new policy rather than treating existing community reserves as daily bonus funds.

Local tests use synthetic keys, mocked RPC/chain senders, and recorded event fixtures. They prove arithmetic, cutoff boundaries, incomplete-history rejection, attribution, funding isolation, immutable manifests, and replay behavior. A live daily bonus payout requires a new policy-bearing mint, qualified daily activity, signed community funding and finalized recipient evidence. Rendering the dashboard or passing these local tests does not establish that live payout.
