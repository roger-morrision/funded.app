## Findings that affect Mainnet

1. **Deployment blocker — router build and destinations.** The agency instructions and two fixed historical QA recovery authorizations share the `devnet-v3-program` build flag. The agency buyback code also embeds the Devnet platform mint and pool. The default build omits the agency entrypoints. Prepare a production build with reviewed mint, pool and program IDs, enumerate its exported instructions, and prove that QA recovery is absent before deploying it. [Router entrypoints](../contracts/funded-fee-router/programs/funded-fee-router/src/lib.rs) · [QA authorizations](../contracts/funded-fee-router/programs/funded-fee-router/src/instructions/recover_qa_v3.rs)
2. **High-impact trust assumption — buyback signer.** The agency program checks signer authority, budget, freshness, the approved pool and token accounts, and the swap/burn outcome. For market-cap milestones the worker supplies the USD observation, its hash and the minimum acceptable token output. A compromised authority could assert that a milestone was reached and choose a weak price limit while staying within the on-chain budget. Production approval needs an explicit signer/custody design and either independently enforced price limits or documented acceptance of this exposure. [Agency buyback](../contracts/funded-fee-router/programs/funded-fee-router/src/instructions/agency_buyback_v3.rs)
3. **Verification gap fixed locally — CI coverage.** The contract workflow previously ran manually and tested only the default router. The updated workflow covers contract-changing pushes and pull requests, builds each binary separately, exercises Devnet-only instructions and records all three executable hashes. Escrow negative tests now check specific policy errors. The hosted workflow still needs to pass after publication. [Workflow](../.github/workflows/contracts.yml)
4. **Dependency review.** OSV matched seven advisories across 421 public packages in the locked Rust workspace. Of 144 packages on the SBF normal-dependency tree (including proc macros), the only match was the unmaintained `bincode 1.3.3`. Two timing advisories affect `crypto-bigint 0.5.5` and `ctutils 0.4.2` outside that tree. The other four matches are maintenance notices for `ansi_term`, `derivative`, `libsecp256k1` and `paste`. Review host/test dependency upgrades separately and reproduce all three binaries after any lockfile change. [Bincode](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2025-0141.html) · [crypto-bigint](https://osv.dev/vulnerability/RUSTSEC-2026-0387) · [ctutils](https://osv.dev/vulnerability/RUSTSEC-2026-0385)

The source review traced router initialization and upgrade-owner checks, protected and agency withdrawals, budget arithmetic, vault and recipient bindings, reward and community proof handling, claim replay, buyback CPI accounts, and escrow amount, mint extensions, time and destination. The 20% limit applies to the recorded creator launch purchase. The 1% app trading fee has separate transaction-builder and receipt verification. This pass did not repeat the paid Devnet trades, the ordinary 5 SOL trigger, historical migration reconstruction or the real X sign-in. Formal verification, an independent line-by-line audit, adversarial fuzzing and a production custody exercise remain open.

## Devnet remediation

The next router release excludes the historical QA recovery entrypoint. Both buyback entrypoints require the executor and the configured price signer to sign the same transaction. The application reconciles previous submissions before pausing new orders when the price service is absent. The escrow verifies the exact token balance changes on release. The local bytecode tests exercise these controls, including rejection of a signer change by an unauthorized wallet. The router executable SHA-256 is `75e9a5cda0ec5a0f9dc03518d9b82aa404eafda3c4969f7d2284bd7fbd20c14d`. The escrow executable SHA-256 is `402e45cc78d7f9372d0a9aa8e02cf5a192411082f4589cb2d5a98ab9505b001b`.

To activate buybacks, a separate custodian must validate the Devnet genesis, instruction accounts, allowed pool, direction, observation freshness, independently sourced price, spend limit and minimum output before signing. The upgrade authority and the incoming price signer must both sign `configure_buyback_price_signer`. Configure the public key through `FUNDED_DEVNET_BUYBACK_PRICE_SIGNER` and the HTTPS signing endpoint through `FUNDED_DEVNET_BUYBACK_PRICE_SIGNER_URL`. The endpoint receives the unsigned versioned transaction as base64 with its program ID and cluster and returns a base64 detached Ed25519 signature over the serialized Solana message. The private price key belongs in the independent custodian's environment. Until that operator is available, leave the configuration empty.
