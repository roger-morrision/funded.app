## Browser journeys added

1. Shared Explore filters load from URL; editing updates the share link; saved search selection survives reload and deletion removes the saved entry.
2. Mobile Explore controls fit; an unavailable service status can be retried.
3. IndexedDB images are bound to their saved settings and deleted correctly.
4. A real prepared PNG and launch settings survive reload and restore; consent resets. Editing while an image restore is delayed preserves the newer form, and deleting prevents subsequent restoration.
5. An injected wallet rejecting connection can retry. Account and network events reset launch consent. The fixture cannot sign or broadcast transactions.
6. Hiding a chat wallet persists through reload, and showing hidden wallets restores its messages.

The model/HTTP suites additionally exercise launch cancellation, uncertain submissions, expired confirmation recovery, partial success, replay protections, fee recipient validation, referral privacy, payout evidence and signed ephemeral transactions against mocked RPC responses.

## Harness corrections

- Market scanning now looks up the canonical pool before scanning curve signatures. The hardening RPC fixture supplies a valid account-info response and verifies both concurrent readers share exactly one lookup and one scan.
- Legacy source assertions now verify the fixed 90-day community claim policy, explicit preview wallet connection, the shared full-address copy control, and current outage wording.
- Image accessibility checks retain the guard against stale asynchronous image preparation clearing newer input.
- Browser scripts accept `CHROMIUM_PATH` while preserving their existing Chrome default. Playwright uses one worker and allows 15 seconds for assertions under shared-host contention; tests continue to fail on uncaught exceptions. Earlier startup-only failures during concurrent browser work passed on clean rerun.
- The jackpot fixture explicitly scrolls the card into view before asserting amount animation; offscreen animation is intentionally suspended. The corrected fixture passes funding-proof loss and recovery.

No mainnet action was performed. Optional feature checks and external dependency failures must not be interpreted as production certification.
