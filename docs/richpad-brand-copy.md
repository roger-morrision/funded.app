# richpad.fun brand and benefit copy

Slogan: **Build together. Share the rewards.**

Home headline: **One community. More ways to earn.**

The gold stepped R and mint leg form the app mark. Use `public/richpad-mark.svg` for app-owned branding; the favicon uses the same artwork. Other projects keep their own logos and metadata. The existing illustrated guides remain separate artwork.

Lead with the benefits for creators, eligible holders, traders and referrers. Use **Rewards for every role**, not a promise that every visitor receives a payout. Keep the asset, eligibility, funding and claim conditions clear in program details. Navigation and financial action labels stay short and explicit.
