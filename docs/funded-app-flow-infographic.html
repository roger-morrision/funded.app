04
Ongoing token support

Two buyback engines. Two different tokens.

A buyback is counted only after a protected purchase, burn instruction, and finalized supply change.

CURRENT FEE POLICY

Platform $FUNDED buyback

5% of collected creator fees

Accrues in a dedicated vault. Once the aggregate trigger and market checks pass, the worker buys and burns $FUNDED.

  1. Fee claim funds the vault
  2. Protected swap buys $FUNDED
  3. BurnChecked reduces $FUNDED supply

Normal aggregate trigger: 5 SOL. Unspent funds remain pending.

INCLUDED · EXECUTION CONDITIONAL

AI marketing agency for the launched coin

2% agency budget

All three included agents split this dedicated budget equally. Their actions support the project’s own coin.

Agent 1 · Awareness: verified platform X promotion, at most once per coin per 24 h; currently paused.
Agent 2 · $100k: one own-coin buyback and burn at verified USD market cap ≥ $100,000.
Agent 3 · $300k: one own-coin buyback and burn at verified USD market cap ≥ $300,000.

Normal agency launches and execution acceptance remain gated; a selected agent or market-cap reading is not a completed action.

Who benefits, and when

A stakeholder view across the full journey

Benefits are opportunities governed by each coin’s saved policy, eligibility, funding, and verified receipts.

Coin creator · launch → feesChoose promotion, reach discovery, and direct 80% of collected creator fees to the chosen recipient mix.
Coin holders · trading → rewardsReceive eligible SOL holder rewards after the funded payout cycle and verified sampling.
Named X account · fees → claimClaim its saved SOL share after identity, wallet, and funding checks.
Referrers · fee settlementQualified uplines receive the saved referral allocation; direct referrers can also compete in Daily bonus.

New-coin community · milestonesEvery new token includes X promotion and both own-coin buyback agents. Actions require verified funding and saved milestones.
$FUNDED ecosystem · launch & feesPaid-tier burns and the separate 5% fee-funded buyback can reduce $FUNDED supply when confirmed.
Platform · ongoingReceives its operations allocation and separate disclosed service charges to support the product.
